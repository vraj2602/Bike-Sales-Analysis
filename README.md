# 🚴  Global Bike Sales Performance: Strategic Revenue & Customer Analytics Dashboard
A dynamic, interactive data visualization tool built to explore sales performance for a global bicycle retailer—focusing on regional growth, product line profitability, demographic efficiency, and customer segmentation.


## 📝 Short Description / Purpose
The **Global Bike Sales Analysis Dashboard** is a comprehensive Power BI report designed to provide executive leadership with actionable insights into their global distribution network. By analyzing historical sales orders, product structures, and customer profiles (leveraging the classic AdventureWorks dataset schema), this tool identifies high-performing geographic hubs, evaluates top product categories (Bikes, Components, Clothing, Accessories), and monitors profit margins to optimize supply chain and targeted marketing strategies.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
The analysis and dashboard were built using the following tools and technologies:
* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main platform for creating the multi-page interactive report and visual storytelling layout.
* 📂 **Power Query** – Facilitated the ETL process, connecting, cleaning, and joining multiple relational source files (Customers, Products, Sales, Territory) into a unified star schema.
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used for advanced analytical calculations including Year-over-Year (YoY) growth, Profit Margin %, rolling averages, and revenue KPIs.

## 💡 Business Impact & Insights
* **Territory Strategy:** Pinpoints geographical market leaders while highlighting emerging international secondary growth territories with dense order volumes.
* **Product Mix Optimization:** Identifies specific high-margin accessories and core bike models that act as the primary revenue anchors, facilitating data-backed inventory planning.
* **Demographic Target Alignment:** Segments purchase behaviors to better tailor promotional campaigns and pricing strategies across differing consumer demographics.
* **Operational Streamlining:** Provides visibility into distribution clusters, helping optimize regional warehouse inventory levels based on localized demand trends.
